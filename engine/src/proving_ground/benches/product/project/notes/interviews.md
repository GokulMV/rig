# Customer interviews: getting paid

## I1: Priya, studio owner, 6 people
"Half my clients pay late. I send reminders by hand from my inbox and I always forget the second one."
"I'd love the reminders to just go out on their own, politely, and stop when they pay."
Uses bank transfer; clients sometimes pay part of an invoice and she loses track of what's left.

## I2: Marco, freelance developer
"Partial payments are a mess. A client paid 40% and the invoice still showed 'unpaid' to them."
"I want to see who owes me what, today, without a spreadsheet."
Would pay for card payments if fees were shown up front.

## I3: Dana, agency finance lead, 30 people
"Reminders matter most. We chase 40 invoices a month by hand."
"Our clients want to pay by card from the email. Bank details in a PDF slow everything down."
"A chatbot? No. Our clients don't want to chat with a bot about money."

## I4: Sam, two-person design shop
"I'd like a 'pay now' button in the invoice email."
"Late fees would help, but I'm scared of annoying clients, so only if I can turn them off per client."

## I5: Lee, consultancy partner
"I need to know which clients are habitually late before I take on more work from them."
"Automatic reminders, absolutely. Partial payments, yes. AI anything, I don't care."

## Internal note
