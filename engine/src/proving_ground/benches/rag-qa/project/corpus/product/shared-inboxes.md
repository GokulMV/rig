# Shared inboxes

Updated: 2025-12-04

A shared inbox collects the tickets for one address or channel, such as support@ or billing@. Agents can be assigned to any number of inboxes.

A workspace on the Team plan can have up to 10 shared inboxes. Business and Enterprise workspaces get more; see the plan table in [pricing](../policies/pricing.md).

Tickets can be moved between inboxes without losing their history.
