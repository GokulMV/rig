# Brindle Desk overview

Updated: 2026-02-01

Brindle Desk is a helpdesk made by Brindle Systems. It turns customer email, chat and forms into tickets that a team works from shared inboxes.

There are three plans: Team, Business and Enterprise. Prices and limits are in [pricing](../policies/pricing.md) and [limits](limits.md). New workspaces start with a free trial, described in [onboarding](../onboarding.md).
