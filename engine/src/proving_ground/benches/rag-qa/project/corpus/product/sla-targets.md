# Support response targets

Updated: 2026-02-01

First-response targets for tickets you send to Brindle Systems support, by priority and plan.

| Priority | Team | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Urgent | 4 hours | 2 hours | 1 hour |
| High | 8 hours | 4 hours | 2 hours |
| Normal | 24 hours | 12 hours | 8 hours |
| Low | 48 hours | 24 hours | 24 hours |

Targets count only within support hours, described in [support hours](../policies/support-hours.md).
