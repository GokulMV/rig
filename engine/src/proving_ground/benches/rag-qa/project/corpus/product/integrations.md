# Integrations

Updated: 2026-02-14

Brindle Desk connects to Pingroom (team chat), Tracklane (issue tracking) and Ledgerly (invoicing).

Pingroom posts a message when a ticket is escalated. Tracklane links a ticket to an issue. Ledgerly shows a customer's open invoices beside their ticket.

Webhooks are retried up to 6 times over 24 hours before they are marked failed.
