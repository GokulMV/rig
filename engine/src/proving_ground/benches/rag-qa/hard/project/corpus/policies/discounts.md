# Discounts

Updated: 2026-04-01

Brindle Systems offers three discount programmes. Apply through the in-app help button; proof of status is needed.

| Programme | Discount on the plan price | Plans | Agents covered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nonprofit | 50% | Team, Business | Up to 20 |
| Education | 30% | Team, Business, Enterprise | All |
| Startup | 25% ¹ | Team, Business | Up to 10 |

¹ The startup discount lasts for the first 12 months of the subscription.

Agents beyond the number a programme covers pay the full plan price. Discounts apply to the plan price only, never to add-ons. A workspace can use one programme at a time.
