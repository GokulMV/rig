# Add-ons

Updated: 2025-10-01

Add-ons are bought per workspace and billed with the rest of the subscription.

| Add-on | Price | Billed per | Plans |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insight (advanced reporting) | $6 | agent per month ¹ | Business, Enterprise |
| Extra contacts pack (25,000 contacts) | $25 | pack per month | Team, Business |

¹ Insight is billed for every agent in the workspace, with a minimum of 5 agents.
