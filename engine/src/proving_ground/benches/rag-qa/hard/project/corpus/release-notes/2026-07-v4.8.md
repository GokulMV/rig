# Release 4.8

Updated: 2026-07-14

Version 4.8 introduces sandbox workspaces. Sandbox data is reset every 14 days.

Failed webhooks are now retried up to 10 times over a retry window of 48 hours (it was 6 times over 24 hours).

Contacts per workspace on the Team plan rise to 25,000 (it was 10,000). Business and Enterprise limits are unchanged.

Each knowledge base article now keeps its last 50 versions (it was 20).
