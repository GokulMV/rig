# Sandbox workspaces

Updated: 2026-09-22

A sandbox workspace is a private copy of your workspace's settings, automation rules and saved replies, without tickets or contacts. Use it to try changes before you make them live.

Sandbox data is reset every 30 days. An admin can copy changes from a sandbox to the live workspace. Sandboxes are an add-on; prices are in [add-ons](add-ons.md).
