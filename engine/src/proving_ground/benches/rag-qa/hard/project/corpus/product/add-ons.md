# Add-ons

Updated: 2026-09-20

Add-ons are bought per workspace and billed with the rest of the subscription. Prices exclude tax. Polyglot has its own page: [Polyglot](translations.md).

| Add-on | Price | Billed per | Plans |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insight (advanced reporting) | $8 | agent per month ¹ | Business, Enterprise ² |
| Extra contacts pack (50,000 contacts) | $30 | pack per month | Team, Business |
| Sandbox workspace | $99 | sandbox per month ³ | Business, Enterprise |

¹ Insight is billed for every agent in the workspace, with a minimum of 10 agents.
² Enterprise workspaces on annual billing get Insight at no charge.
³ Every Enterprise workspace includes one sandbox at no charge; each further sandbox is $99 per month.

With Insight, report data is kept for 60 months instead of 24, and it can still be filtered by agent. Sandboxes are described in [sandbox workspaces](sandbox.md).
