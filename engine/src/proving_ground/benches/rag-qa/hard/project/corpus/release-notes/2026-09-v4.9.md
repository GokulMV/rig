# Release 4.9

Updated: 2026-09-08

Version 4.9 makes dashboards refresh every 5 minutes on the Business and Enterprise plans. On the Team plan dashboards still refresh every 15 minutes.

Failed webhooks are now retried up to 8 times; the retry window of 48 hours is unchanged. Version 4.8 raised retries to 10, which put too much load on some customers' endpoints.
