# Billing: build or buy

- We bill 4,200 customers monthly. The current scripts break about twice a month; each break costs about 20 hours of engineering.
- **Build**: 3 engineers for 9 months (fully loaded 15,000 per engineer-month = 405,000), then 1 engineer ongoing (180,000 a year).
- **Buy** platform A: 180,000 a year, 3 months to migrate with 2 engineers (90,000 once).
- **Buy** platform B: 0.8% of billed revenue (revenue 30 million a year, so 240,000 a year), 6 weeks to migrate (45,000 once); takes over payments too.
- Sales wants usage-based pricing within 12 months; only platform A and an in-house build support it today.
- Finance: no new headcount this year.
