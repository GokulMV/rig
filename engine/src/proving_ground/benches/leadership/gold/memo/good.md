# Billing: buy platform A

## Options
- **Build in-house**: 405,000 to build over 9 months, then 180,000 a year. Needs 3 engineers when we can't hire this year, and the broken scripts keep costing about 40 hours a month until it ships.
- **Buy platform A**: 180,000 a year plus 90,000 once to migrate in 3 months with 2 engineers. Supports usage-based pricing.
- **Buy platform B**: 240,000 a year (0.8% of 30 million) plus 45,000 once, live in 6 weeks, takes over payments. No usage-based pricing, which Sales needs within 12 months.

## Recommendation
Buy platform A. Over two years it costs 450,000 against 630,000 to build (405,000 plus 15 months at 15,000) and 525,000 for B, it supports usage-based pricing, and it needs no new hires. First step: start the 3-month migration with two engineers next month.

## What would change my mind
If platform B ships usage-based pricing within 6 months, its faster migration wins. If revenue fell below 22.5 million, B's 0.8% would cost less than A's 180,000 a year.
