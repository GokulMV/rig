# Refund policy

1. **Monthly plans** (Basic $20/month, Pro $100/month): a full refund of the last charge if asked
   within 14 days of that charge. After 14 days: no refund (decline), but the plan can be cancelled.
2. **Annual plans** (Basic $200/year, Pro $1,000/year): within 30 days of the charge, a full refund.
   From day 31 to day 90, a partial refund of the unused whole months (annual price / 12 per month).
   After day 90: decline.
3. **Outages**: if the customer was affected by a listed outage (OUTAGES below), add a credit of 10%
   of one month's price; this is on top of any refund and does not change the decision.
4. **Anything over $500**, or a customer threatening legal action, or a request outside these rules:
   escalate to a person. Never promise an amount when escalating (AMOUNT: $0.00).
5. Replies end with `DECISION: refund|partial|escalate|decline` and `AMOUNT: $X.XX` (the refund, not the credit).

OUTAGES: 2026-03-02 (6 hours, all regions).
