# Fewer empty chairs, fewer double bookings

Every clinic manager knows the morning call: a patient cancels, the slot sits empty, and the people on your waitlist never hear about it. Smart Scheduling fills that slot for you. When someone cancels, it texts the people on your waitlist automatically.

No-shows cost you the same way. In our beta, 14 clinics cut no-shows from 18% to 11% over three months.

Smart Scheduling also blocks double booking across rooms and practitioners, so two patients can't be booked into the same room, or with the same practitioner, at the same time.

It works with the Google Calendar or Outlook you already use. If your clinic still runs on a paper diary, it won't connect to that.

After the beta, Smart Scheduling costs $29 per practitioner per month. Until the beta ends on 30 June 2027 it's free, for up to 50 clinics in the US and Canada.
