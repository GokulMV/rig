# Smart Scheduling: facts
- Fills cancelled slots from a waitlist by text message, automatically.
- Beta clinics cut no-shows from 18% to 11% over three months (14 clinics).
- Blocks double booking across rooms and practitioners.
- Price: $29 per practitioner per month. Free during the beta, which ends 2027-06-30. To join, clinics reply to the launch post.
- The beta is limited to 50 clinics in the US and Canada.
- Works with Google Calendar and Outlook. No integration with paper diaries.
